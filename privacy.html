Privacy policy

This policy covers Household Mirror, a private display used by the members of one household. It was last updated on November 22, 2026.

What the app accesses

When a household member signs in with Google, the app asks for read-only access to two things:

  • Google Calendar: the events on that person's calendars.
  • Google Tasks: that person's task lists and tasks.

The app cannot create, change or delete calendar events or tasks.

How the data is used

Events and tasks are shown on the mirror's screen to the person they belong to. They are used for nothing else.

Where the data is kept

The sign-in token and the events and tasks it fetches stay on the mirror, a small computer inside the home. They are not copied to any server, and there is no analytics or advertising.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A household member can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the mirror removes the stored sign-in.

Contact

Questions go to the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen for Household Mirror.
