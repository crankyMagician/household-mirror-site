Household Mirror

Household Mirror is a private display in one home. It is a mirror with a screen behind the glass, built on the open source MagicMirror² project.

What it does

The mirror shows the people who live in that home their own day: the time, the weather, upcoming calendar events and task lists. Each person signs in with their own Google account, and the mirror shows that person's calendar and tasks when it recognizes them.

Who can use it

Only the members of that household. It is one device in one home. It is not a public service and it has no sign-up.

How it uses Google data

It asks for read-only access to Google Calendar and Google Tasks. It reads upcoming events and open tasks and shows them on the screen. It cannot change or delete anything. The privacy policy has the details.
